## Canonical dependency findings

`authority.reference` is optional, private, max 191. It has no dedicated consuming moderation, notification or publication logic. Generic canonical persistence would retain it in private JSON if supplied. Its UI/config/label/CSS mapping is removed; schema, persistence, historical values and certified fixtures are retained unchanged.

112 is stored as `authority.status=yes` and `authority.type=public_service`, the existing broad public-service classification. It is NOT a new persistent 112 discriminator, and it does not imply police or carabinieri. No further responding force is required. A distinct canonical 112 value would require a separate contract decision.

Reward is retained. Choosing `none` avoids the entire conditional reward branch. The branch uses canonical amount/conditions/expiry/confirmation and downstream amount/expiry storage. It remains a candidate for a separate MVP simplification decision; it was not silently removed.

## Consent boundaries

Only truthfulness/good faith and rights to content are grouped in one UI declaration. Their canonical booleans are still independently represented. Publication rules, terms and privacy acknowledgement remain three distinct controls with original validation/storage. Contact preference remains separate and mutually exclusive under its existing three-valued contract. No privacy, contractual or optional communication acceptance is auto-selected by the grouped declaration. This conservative UI grouping is not a legal certification of policies.
