# BadAround — Event SEO v2, 12 November 2026

Applies to the current badaround.it test installation and all subsequent event publications. Supersedes the municipality-only URL generator, while preserving the stable public URL identity and freezing URLs after first completed publication. No event deletion or reset is part of this work.

## Public source and publication timing

Only canonical public taxonomy, existing approved public vehicle fields, and the sealed public-details snapshot may supply SEO. No source report reads in the renderer. A private exact address is never a fallback. First controlled B3 finalizes the URL after the declared public snapshot is signed, within the same publication transaction. Re-publication and later detail edits do not change a frozen URL.

## URL composition

`/eventi/{specific-event-or-object-state}-{municipality}-{entity-if-known}-{approved-place-if-relevant}-{public-street-or-zone}/`

Components are optional except event and municipality. Prefer public street, otherwise approved neighborhood/locality. Do not infer neighborhood from street names. Omit civic numbers, plates, names, contacts and occurrence dates. Use lowercase sanitized hyphen-separated components, a 160-byte base budget without cutting components, and a stable opaque public-identity suffix for collisions. Existing test events may be explicitly regenerated by an authorized administrator during maintenance, with a backup and audit and without generating old-slug redirects.

| Family | Required semantics | Optional approved specificity |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicles | Theft, parked damage, hit and run, parts theft, observation or other event | Make/model and public street or area |
| Property | Theft, attempted break-in, intrusion, vandalism, suspicious behavior or other event | Private home, condominium, shop, office, garage or other compatible public place fact |
| Hazards | Exact canonical risk subtype | Public street or approved area; do not choose a more specific hazard from an ambiguous combined subtype |
| Public spaces | Waste, surface damage, lighting, signage, urban furnishings/green or other service issue | Public street or approved area |
| Animals | Missing, sighted/found, injured or at risk | Approved species; no invented breed, owner or condition |
| Objects/documents | Specific object subtype and explicit lost/found state | Public street or area; absent state stays neutral |

## Editorial roles

One H1 identifies the reported event, entity and location. Title is concise and may prioritize a shorter wording; the brand suffix occurs once. Description summarizes the event, location and known occurrence date, with a contextual invitation. Budgets are editorial constraints, not Google display guarantees. Dates accept validated ISO or explicit Italian legacy input; never substitute submission date for occurrence date.

H2 sections serve different intents: identification, circumstances, images, location and community updates. H3 separates address, occurrence and map within the location section. There is no random synonym rotation or keyword-density target. Preserve uncertainty: suspected, reported, sighted or combined subtypes must not become proven theft, confirmed fire or verified recovery. Optional sections retain their existing content conditions.

Original moderated narrative is preserved. Only the exact system-generated generic narrative may be replaced at render time by a public-fact summary. Missing facts are omitted, never invented.

## Images and head metadata

Main gallery alt describes an attachment to the specific report and includes its photo ordinal, without asserting visually unverified features. Decorative placeholders and duplicate thumbnail images retain empty alt; thumbnail buttons have accessible labels. Open Graph and Twitter share Title, description and canonical URL. Only already-approved public gallery media may become social images or JSON-LD images; no private media and no large generic placeholder as a purported event photo.

The JSON-LD graph describes WebPage, Report and BreadcrumbList, with consistent canonical IDs, optional publication/modification dates and approved images. Do not use schema.org Event for thefts, hazards or lost-property reports, nor invent private Person/author identities. An installed external SEO owner suppresses duplicate metadata. All critical text is server-rendered.

## Test migration and release

Maintenance-only administrator endpoint requires moderation/edit capabilities, explicit apply, expected previous slug, InnoDB transactions, a prior title/slug backup, collision handling and an audit record. It does not send email, publish social content, modify reports, change geographic projections or delete content. Normal live publications continue to freeze URLs.

Google chooses its own title/snippet and indexing. This contract does not guarantee ranking, rich results or immediate indexing; the site remains in maintenance/noindex until the separate launch decision.
